    How it works

    Tap any state, province or country you’ve been to. The Been there | Want to go switch in the corner of each map changes what a tap marks; places you want to go show up striped. Tap a place again to clear it. Your marks save on this phone or computer automatically, with no account and nothing uploaded.

    Install it

    On iPhone, open this page in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android or a computer, use the Install button up top (or your browser’s install option). Install first, then start marking; on iPhone, marks made in a Safari tab don’t carry over to the installed app.

    Notes and ratings

    Tap a name in the lists below a map to give it a rating from 1 to 5, the year you went and a short note. The Ratings map style colors the map by your stars. Only you see notes and ratings, unless you turn on the notes link in Share.

    Cities

    Zoom into either map and cities appear as dots. U.S. and Canadian cities show on both maps, including every state and provincial capital. Marking a city also marks its country, and a U.S. or Canadian city also marks its state or province. Search finds any of about 3,300 cities, even ones too small to show yet. Cities need one visit while online before they work offline.

    Share

    This is how your map gets to your other devices and to other people.

    • Share → Create link gives you a view link that shows your map only: where you’ve been and want to go, never your notes. Anyone can open it; nobody can change your map with it.
    • A second link, map with notes, stays off until you switch it on. It shows your ratings, years and notes too, and stops working if you switch it off or reset it.
    • To add your own laptop or tablet, open Share here and scan the QR code with that device’s camera. After that, both stay in sync, even if you mark things offline.
    • Only open an edit link on your own devices, since anyone with it can change your map. If one ever ends up with the wrong person, Reset edit access shuts it off.

    Backup

    A manual copy, separate from Share. The code holds your map only (been there and want to go); the backup file has everything, notes and ratings included. Paste the code or pick the file on any device to bring it back. Older codes starting with atlas1: still restore. The atlas reminds you to make a backup now and then if you aren’t using Share.

    Backup & restore

    Your marks live on this device. Copy the code or download a file to keep a backup or move your marks to another phone or computer. Paste a code and press Restore, or choose a backup file, to bring marks back. The code holds your map only; the file also keeps your notes, ratings and years.

    Share & sync

    Create a link to keep this map in step on your other phones and computers, and to show it to friends. A copy of your marks and name is stored on atlas.elijahpelton.com. Anyone with the view link can look at the map; only devices you add can change it. You can stop syncing or delete the copy at any time.
